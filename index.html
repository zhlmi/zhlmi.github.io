DuiCi · Voice Prompter Privacy Policy

对词 · 声控提词器(以下简称“本应用”)尊重并保护你的隐私。本应用不需要注册账号,不收集任何个人信息。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 本应用不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息、音频、文稿或使用数据。
  • 本应用没有账号系统,不需要登录。
  • 本应用不包含广告、第三方统计或任何第三方 SDK,不进行跨应用追踪。

2. 麦克风与语音识别

  • 本应用在提词时使用麦克风,以便跟随你的语速滚动文字。
  • 语音识别通过 Apple 系统提供的端侧语音识别(SpeechAnalyzer)在你的设备本地完成,音频不会发送到任何服务器。
  • 本应用不录音、不保存音频,识别出的文字只在内存中临时使用,不写入存储。
  • 首次使用某种语言时,系统可能会从 Apple 下载语音识别模型,该过程由 iOS 系统完成。

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据只保存在你的设备上,存放于本应用的沙盒中,不会离开设备:

  • 你导入或粘贴的文稿及标红信息;
  • 显示设置(字号、镜像、语言等);
  • 最近 50 次提词的跟读记录:只包含时间、行号、置信度和跟读决策,不包含任何文字或音频。删除文稿时,对应的记录一并删除。

删除本应用即可清除以上全部数据。

4. 局域网显示端(可选功能)

  • 只有当你在“设置 › 显示端”中主动开启时,本应用才会在你的局域网内提供一个只读网页,让同一网络中的浏览器查看提词画面。
  • 开启后,提词画面(包括文稿文字)会通过局域网发送给打开网址的浏览器,不经过任何服务器。网址中包含随机口令,你可以随时更换网址,关闭功能后即停止发送。
  • 该网页通过局域网明文传输,请只在你信任的网络中使用。
  • 首次开启时,系统会请求“本地网络”权限,你可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

5. 权限

本应用仅使用以下系统权限,且只用于本政策所述的功能:

  • 麦克风:用于语音跟读提词(见第 2 节);
  • 本地网络:用于可选的显示端功能(见第 4 节)。

你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中关闭上述权限。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何个人信息,包括儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更,我们会在本页面更新,并修改上方的更新日期。

DuiCi · Voice Prompter ("the App") respects and protects your privacy. The App requires no account and collects no personal information.

1. Information we don't collect

  • The App does not collect, upload or share any personal information, audio, scripts or usage data.
  • There is no account system and no sign-in.
  • The App contains no ads, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

  • The App uses the microphone while prompting so the text can follow your speaking pace.
  • Speech is recognized locally on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Audio is never sent to any server.
  • The App does not record or save audio. Recognized text is used temporarily in memory and is never written to storage.
  • The first time you use a language, iOS may download a speech model from Apple. This is handled by the system.

3. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device, inside the App's sandbox:

  • Scripts you import or paste, and the words you mark;
  • Display settings (font size, mirroring, language, etc.);
  • Follow logs for your last 50 sessions, containing only time, line numbers, confidence and follow decisions — no text or audio. They are deleted together with the script.

Deleting the App removes all of this data.

4. Local network display (optional)

  • Only when you turn it on in Settings › Display does the App serve a view-only web page on your local network, so browsers on the same network can see the prompter.
  • While on, the prompter view (including script text) is sent over your local network to browsers that open the link, never through a server. The link contains a random token, you can generate a new link at any time, and turning the feature off stops sending.
  • The page is sent unencrypted over your local network. Use it only on networks you trust.
  • iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time; you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

5. Permissions

The App uses only the following system permissions, solely for the features described in this policy:

  • Microphone: for voice-follow prompting (Section 2);
  • Local Network: for the optional display feature (Section 4).

You can turn these off in iOS Settings at any time.

6. Children's privacy

The App collects no personal information, including from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
